About Us

CareerHatch is designed to support all jobseekers, from students and fresh graduates to experienced workers, by providing a safe and empowering space to find opportunities. We verify employers, provide real-time support, and help users avoid fake listings. Whether applying online or walking into a local office, we are here to guide you every step of the way with honesty, tools, and care.

Meet the Founder

Hi, I’m Vinceneil Ursal, the person behind CareerHatch.

This project started with no money, no big team, just an idea. My goal was simple: to help people like me find real jobs without falling into scams. It’s exhausting sending out applications and getting no reply, or worse, falling into fake offers.

CareerHatch started small. I worked on it piece by piece, coding, designing, and planning, sometimes with little to no sleep. I built it while figuring out everything on my own. It wasn’t easy, but I believed it was worth it.

What used to be just “Career Patch” is now growing into CareerHatch. A job platform for both local and online opportunities, built for real people with real dreams. It’s not perfect yet, but it’s a start.

I’m still working on it today. Improving, learning, and pushing forward. I know this can help someone out there, and that’s all the reason I need to keep going.

Thank you for supporting CareerHatch.
