About CareerHatch

Built from a real problem experienced by students and jobseekers.

CareerHatch is a job platform created to make job searching safer and more straightforward. It focuses on clarity, verification, and guidance instead of overwhelming users with misleading listings.

The goal is simple: help people find real opportunities and understand what to expect before applying.

What CareerHatch Provides

Practical tools designed to support jobseekers from start to finish.

Verified Job Listings

Employers are checked to reduce scams and misleading offers.

Guided Applications

Clear steps on what documents and skills are needed.

Career Insights

Tips from real experiences shared by other jobseekers.

Community Support

A space to learn, ask questions, and grow together.

How It Started

A college project that aimed to solve a real-world issue.

CareerHatch started as a college requirement to build a website from scratch and identify a specific problem to solve.

Research revealed that many jobseekers struggle with fake listings, unclear job requirements, and lack of guidance.

The project evolved into CareerHatch — a platform focused on safety, transparency, and support.
