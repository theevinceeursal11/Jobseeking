Learn More About CareerHatch

CareerHatch is a job platform made for real people—students, graduates, vocational workers, freelancers, and local jobseekers. Whether you’re applying online or in-person at a local shop, SEAConnect helps you find verified opportunities and avoid scams.

Why We Created CareerHatch

Many of us have been scammed or ignored while looking for jobs. From fake online offers to shady walk-in promises, the job search can be risky and frustrating. CareerHatch was built to make job hunting safer, clearer, and more supportive for everyone.

Problems in the Job Market

  • Online job posts often have fake employers or hidden fees.
  • Many walk-in applicants don’t know if the job is real or still available.
  • New graduates and students often feel lost and unsupported.
  • Scams and unpaid gigs continue to hurt freelancers and local workers.
  • There’s no central place where people can ask questions or get guidance.

Our Goals

  • To connect people with real, verified jobs—both online and local.
  • To protect jobseekers from scams and fake offers.
  • To give guidance and support to new applicants who don’t know where to start.
  • To help employers find honest and qualified workers more easily.

Our Vision

We dream of a future where looking for a job doesn’t feel scary. A future where everyone, no matter their background, can find honest work and grow with confidence. SEAConnect wants to be more than just a job board—we want to be your starting point, your safety net, and your career companion.

Because everyone deserves a fair chance.
