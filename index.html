About CareerHatch

Built to Protect and Empower Jobseekers

CareerHatch was created to solve one major problem—unsafe and unreliable job hunting. We connect jobseekers with verified employers, remove scams from the process, and provide tools that help people grow their careers with confidence.

Whether you're a student, fresh graduate, or professional, CareerHatch gives you a safe place to apply, learn, and connect.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CareerHatch free to use?

Yes. You can start with our Free Plan and upgrade anytime if you need more features.

How do you verify employers?

All employers go through manual screening and document checks before posting jobs.

Is this for online or local jobs?

Both. CareerHatch supports online, freelance, and local community-based jobs.

Can students and fresh graduates apply?

Absolutely. CareerHatch is designed to support beginners and career shifters.
